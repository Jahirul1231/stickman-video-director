## Output evidence

The owner judged the Roman text-to-video trials relatively consistent. Earlier Egyptian trials exposed character, eye and voice drift; they are not evidence of universally solved continuity. The two chosen showcase clips were checked through local contact sheets for the intended cartoon drawing grammar and historical settings. This publication did not independently perform a new full audio listening test or a fresh cross-era provider run.

Full source films are retained in `assets/readme/style-5-japan.mp4` (about 30 seconds) and `assets/readme/style-5-egypt.mp4` (about 10 seconds). README GIFs use their opening ten seconds at 6 fps; gallery previews are 400×225 and the Japanese hero is 640×360.
